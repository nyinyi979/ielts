How to use every day

1 · Plan firstTask 1: identify two overview features and two detail groups. Task 2: decide the exact answer, then two main ideas with reasons.
2 · Write under timeDo not open the model while drafting. Protect task coverage and clarity before trying to sound sophisticated.
3 · Rewrite one weaknessCompare your decisions with the model, label the error cause, then rewrite your weakest paragraph without looking.
