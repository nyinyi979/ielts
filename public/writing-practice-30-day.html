How to use this pack

Daily timing

  • Task 1: 20 minutes · 150+ words
  • Task 2: 40 minutes · 250+ words
  • Keep 3–5 minutes for checking.

Band 7–7.5 target

  • Clear Task 1 overview
  • Clear Task 2 position
  • Developed main ideas
  • Logical comparisons and paragraphs
  • Controlled vocabulary and grammar

Note: These are original practice tasks based on common IELTS Academic formats and themes, not official live test papers.

Task 1 structure

  1. Introduction: paraphrase.
  2. Overview: 2–3 biggest features.
  3. Details 1: group and compare.
  4. Details 2: remaining comparisons.

Task 2 structure

  1. Introduction: paraphrase + direct answer.
  2. Body 1: idea → explanation → example.
  3. Body 2: idea → explanation → example.
  4. Conclusion: restate position.

Final self-check

Task 1

  • Clear overview?
  • Compared instead of listed?
  • Selected important features?
  • Accurate tense, figures and units?

Task 2

  • Answered every part?
  • Opinion clear throughout?
  • One main job per paragraph?
  • Ideas explained and supported?
  • 250+ words?
